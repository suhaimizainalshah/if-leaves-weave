What it can read

Choose the layout closest to your papers, or leave it on Detect for me. Mixed papers work too: each rule below is always on unless switched off.

Good to know

  • Works best: typed papers with clear numbering, multiple choice with an answer key, and marking schemes numbered like the paper.
  • Needs a check: two-column pages (set them in step 2), questions split across pages, and numbering that restarts in each section (name the sections).
  • Not read as text: diagrams, graphs, tables and equations. Pictures are cropped next to their question, with tools to describe them.

Check how each page will be read

Pages are checked automatically and flagged when they look scanned, two-column or like an answer section. Change any page that should be read differently.
