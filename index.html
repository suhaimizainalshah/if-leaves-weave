    Find and redact

    Finds personal details in the text and marks them for redaction. Check the list, then download: marked pages are flattened so the hidden text is permanently removed.

    Look for
    Redaction report

    A record of what was removed, on which pages and when, with a fingerprint of the redacted PDF. It never includes the removed details.

    Download the redacted PDF first, so the report can include its fingerprint.

    Bates numbering and stamps

    Gives every page a unique number, such as ABC000001, for legal, audit and compliance documents. Number one file or many in sequence.

    Position

    For several files, If Leaves Weave numbers them in name order and downloads a ZIP with an index of the number range in each file.

    Flipbook

    Turns your PDF into a page-turning book: a single web page you can email, put on a website or open offline.

    Quality

    Readers turn pages by clicking, swiping or using the arrow keys, can search the text and jump to any page, and can link to a page (for example …#page-12). To share it as a link, put the file on a website.

    Classroom tools

    Read aloud

    Reads the document out loud, which helps learners who prefer listening or are still building reading confidence.

    Add paper pages

    Insert writing or maths paper after the selected page, or at the end.

    Voice notes

    Record spoken feedback or instructions and save them inside the PDF.

    Voice notes play in PDF apps that show attachments, such as Adobe Acrobat Reader. Browsers’ built-in viewers usually don’t show them.

    Read text (OCR)

    Recognises the words in scanned pages and photos, so you can search, select and copy them.

    The first time, your browser downloads the text-recognition engine and language data (about 5 to 15 MB). Your document itself never leaves your device.
