Study cards

Test yourself, and the cards come back just before you’d forget them. Practice testing and spacing out study are the two techniques with the strongest evidence for helping learners (Dunlosky et al., 2013).

Your cards and progress stay in this browser. Download a copy to move them to another device.

Add a card

Maths: put it between dollar signs, such as $x^2+1$. Ctrl+Enter adds the card.
Add many at once, or import

One card per line, with the front and back separated by | or a tab. Or open a CSV file, such as one exported from the Question bank.
